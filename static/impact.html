Tap a bridge on the map. The model assumes it is closed and recomputes access to hospitals over the mapped road network. It does not say the bridge has failed.

Optional: person-hours and value of time (your own figures)

No traffic counts are invented. Enter a daily vehicle count for the bridge (for example from a road-authority count) to estimate lost hours.

Method, assumptions and sources

    Person-hours = vehicles/day × closure days × occupancy × detour minutes ÷ 60. Population losing access counts people whose nearest mapped hospital moves from within the threshold to beyond it. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
