CHALLENGE 2 · SPACE-ENABLED CRISIS RESPONSE AND CIVIL RESILIENCE

Understand hazards. Find local guidance.

Resilience Watch brings Copernicus and ECMWF evidence into a focused map, alongside local emergency contacts and mapped facility candidates.

Open the application

Available in a current browser on phones, tablets and computers. No installation is required.

Rain & terrain

Watch the next 24 hours of modelled rainfall. Explore where terrain could concentrate runoff, without claiming measured flood-water depth.

Fire evidence

See satellite thermal detections and reported fires separately from weather-based fire danger. Missing reports are never an all-clear.

Local resilience

Find your area's emergency number and official civil-protection links. Check nearby mapped assembly/shelter candidates, subject to coverage.

A research prototype, not an emergency service

No destination is certified safe, open or officially designated by this app. Shelter designation belongs to local or national authorities. No validated safe route or street-level hazard prediction is available.

Facility lookup is currently unreliable and may show no candidates. Satellite and forecast sources can be delayed or unavailable. Follow official instructions in an emergency.

How to try it

Select Open the application. The browser asks for location permission so the map can show your area. You can decline and choose a city instead. Rain, fire and nearby places have separate views.

Your selected coordinates are sent to the hosting service and relevant data providers to retrieve local information. Location is not stored as disk-based history.

Data and limitations

Copernicus and ECMWF provide European evidence; OpenStreetMap, GDACS and FIRMS supplement it. Facility records are not verified shelters, and no destination is certified safe or officially designated by this app.

This independent research prototype is not an official warning or evacuation service and has no EU endorsement. Follow local authorities in an emergency.
