No account, download or installation needed to view the hosted app. Use a current browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

Rain & terrain

Watch the next 24 hours of modelled rainfall. Explore where terrain could concentrate runoff, without claiming measured flood-water depth.

Fire evidence

See satellite thermal detections and reported fires separately from weather-based fire danger. Missing reports are never an all-clear.

Local resilience

Find your area's emergency number and official civil-protection links. Check nearby mapped assembly/shelter candidates, subject to coverage.

A research prototype, not an emergency service

No destination is certified safe, open or officially designated by this app. Shelter designation belongs to local or national authorities. No validated safe route or street-level hazard prediction is available.

Facility lookup is currently unreliable and may show no candidates. Satellite and forecast sources can be delayed or unavailable. Follow official instructions in an emergency.

How to try it

Select Open the prototype. The browser asks for location permission so the map can show your area. You can decline and choose a city instead. Rain, fire and nearby places have separate views; technical detail stays collapsed.

This introduction does not request location. In the live app, your selected coordinates are sent to the hosting service and data providers. Opening a shared link does not reveal the sender's location.

Some in-app email browsers restrict location or installation. If that happens, open the same link in Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Edge. Installation on a home screen is optional.

Send one link, not setup instructions

The link opens this introduction first. It does not include GPS coordinates, a selected city or a previously calculated destination.
